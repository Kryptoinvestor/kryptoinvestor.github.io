PRIN (Pure Income) - Litepaper

The Mathematical Income Model

PRIN employs a mathematical income model running natively on-chain on Arbitrum One. Income generation started on March 1, 2026, with an initial monthly emission of 2.6%.

The Multiplicative Decay

To ensure long-term sustainability, the emission rate decreases relatively by 1% every month. This multiplicative decay naturally limits the maximum supply, mathematically capping it around 18.4 million PRIN, remaining strictly below the hard cap of 21,000,000.

Proportional Distribution

Emissions are distributed via a global index multiplier (the updateIndex function), ensuring all holders benefit proportionally without manual claiming. A 5% founder fee is minted directly to the genesis address upon each update.
