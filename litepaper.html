PRIN (Pure Income) - Litepaper

Introduction

PRIN is a decentralized utility and store-of-value token deployed on the Arbitrum One network. At its core, the project is driven by a sophisticated, mathematically defined income model designed to bridge community liquidity with sustainable, long-term ecosystem incentives.

Immutability & True Decentralization

PRIN is fundamentally designed around fairness and trustless execution. The smart contract contains no admin keys to alter the tokenomics, no blacklist functions, and no manual supply manipulation capabilities. The total supply cap and the emission logic are immutably hardcoded into the Arbitrum blockchain.

The Mathematical Income Model

PRIN employs a mathematical income model running natively on-chain. The mathematical clock for income generation started on March 1, 2026, with an initial monthly emission of 2.6%. Due to the fixed decay model, the monthly emission rate remains highly attractive, staying strictly above 2.0% for the first 2 years, and above 1.0% for the first 8 years.

The Multiplicative Decay

To ensure long-term sustainability, the emission rate decreases relatively by 1% every month. This multiplicative decay naturally limits the maximum supply, mathematically capping it around 18.4 million PRIN, remaining strictly below the hard cap of 21,000,000.

Proportional Distribution

Emissions are distributed via a global index multiplier (the updateIndex function), ensuring all holders benefit proportionally without manual claiming. A 5% founder fee is minted directly to the genesis address upon each update.
